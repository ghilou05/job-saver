# job-saver

A lightweight browser extension that lets you save the job listing you're currently viewing straight to a Notion database, with one click. It pulls the page title and URL automatically, lets you fill in the company and optional notes, and creates a new page in your Notion job tracker via the Notion API.

## Setup

1. Load the extension unpacked in your browser.
2. Open the extension's Options page and enter your Notion integration token and database ID.
3. Open a job listing, click the extension icon, fill in the details, and hit **Save to Notion**.
